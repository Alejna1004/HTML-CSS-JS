What defines birds?

Birds are warm-blooded, feathered vertebrates with wings, hard-shelled eggs, and lightweight skeletons. While most birds can fly, some species like penguins and ostriches are flightless despite having wings. They are a diverse group with a wide range of species and a high metabolic rate.

What makes them special?

Birds are special because they are the only animals with feathers and are defined by unique characteristics like hard-shelled eggs, beaks without teeth, and lightweight, hollow bones. They also share other distinct features such as being warm-blooded, having wings (even if flightless), and a keen sense of sight.
